Model layers (tap for details)
Layer 1 — Base Stats: 2025-26 per-game lines, Basketball-Reference, unadjusted. Layer 2 — Situational Adjustment: per-category nudges (not one flat multiplier) from verified trades/role changes — cross-checked against ESPN's team transaction logs and the depth chart, not just my own read of the roster. Catches things like usage shifting after a teammate leaves, or a season suppressed by injury, plus specific return-date modeling for players with a reported recovery target. Availability Discount (parallel, not sequential): the A–E tier scaling every player's contribution by expected games played. Layer 3 — Schedule Simulation: considered, not built — back-to-back/rest effects fold into the Availability tier instead, and weekly-matchup scheduling is a different, in-season tool, not a draft-prep one. Layer 4 — Monte Carlo: built — tap Impact on any player for a simulated P10–P90 range, sampling games-missed and per-category variance. FG%/FT% are weighted by attempts; turnovers count against you. This is a directional model, not exact math.
